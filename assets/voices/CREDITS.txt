CommandPower unit and announcer audio

Dialogue, procedural nonverbal sounds, processing, and generator code:
Copyright (c) 2026 CommandPower contributors; see the game's LICENSE.

Human unit and command-announcer lines were generated locally using Piper 1.3.0 and the publicly
distributed Piper voice models, then processed into original radio
effects. Neural models, speech tools, and source recordings are not bundled
with the game. No dialogue was sent to an online speech service.

en_US-joe-medium
Model: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/joe/medium
Model card: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/raw/main/en/en_US/joe/medium/MODEL_CARD
Source dataset: https://github.com/OHF-Voice/voice-datasets
The model card identifies the source dataset license as CC0, and notes
fine-tuning from the U.S. English lessac medium voice.

Its code and models are not included in CommandPower's runtime or package.

Faction sound themes — 2026-10-26

Only Human units use spoken dialogue. All 180 Human recordings remain unchanged.
The command announcer also retains its existing recordings and processing.

All 900 non-Human unit responses are original procedural, wordless sounds:
Iron Synod: cybernetic resonance, metallic pulses and network-like harmonics.
Kharuun Brood: living grove, leafy breath and resonant wood.
Heavenly Host: celestial choir, wordless harmony, organ tones and bells.
Infernal Legion: abyssal growl, snarls, rough breath and dark reflections.
Veyr Dominion: crystal resonance, inharmonic chimes and cavernous echoes.

Each faction has 21 unit roles and three variants per supported action; unarmed
units have no attack bank. Unit register and texture vary within the faction
sound theme. No speech recordings or speech models enter these non-Human cues.
The 96 existing original-role wordless recordings, including Seraph Guard's
choir, are preserved byte-for-byte. The other 804 non-Human clips replace the
previous spoken responses. faction_theme_revision.json records protected,
previous and current SHA-256 hashes. original_hashes.json records the current
approved 144 original-role recordings. manifest.json and expansion_manifest.json
record unit identity, action, sound theme and duration; only Human entries
contain dialogue transcripts.

Procedural synthesis and processing run offline using Python, NumPy and
libsndfile. Only rendered stereo Ogg files are used during gameplay.
